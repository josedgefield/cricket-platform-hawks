Load CricHeroes stats (preview)

Open the Hawks CC leaderboard on CricHeroes, pick a tab, copy its table (or a CSV of it) and paste it here. Columns are matched by header name, such as Player, Mat, Inns, NO, Runs, HS, Balls, 4s, 6s, Overs, Maidens, Wkts, Catches, Stumpings and Run outs. The page recalculates rates itself and flags any rate that disagrees with CricHeroes. Names map to members through the alias list, and unknown names are listed for mapping, never added automatically.

The preview appears here. Nothing changes until you press Apply.
