## Drift check

> [!warning] Some drift: stats ahead of finance; data fetching revisited often
> Stats (Phase 3) was built before sign-in and finance (Phases 0–1). The reason holds, because finance waits on club answers, but finance is still the top priority. Getting data in (CricHeroes access, SCA pages, automatic updates, IMPORTHTML) came up five times without a final decision; the requirements only ask for export imports, "last synced" and corrections. The stack change was in scope and the docs were updated, though `docs/13` lagged until this report.

**Advice:** stop extending stats beyond loading SCA data; spend the next session on member sign-in while the club answers Q1–Q3 and Q6.
